# VitalDB supine open versus laparoscopic pneumoperitoneum

Generated 2026-11-27.

## Methods

Compares paired pre-incision (10 min before opstart) to mid-surgery (20-60 min after opstart)
changes in Primus dynamic/static compliance, EtCO2, minute ventilation, alveolar CO2 elimination
proxy (VA * EtCO2 / 0.863), MAP, and HR between supine Open and supine Videoscopic/Robotic cases.

Supine retained candidates: 463. Paired complete: 399 (Open: 277, Laparoscopic/Robotic: 122).

## Net laparoscopic minus open pneumoperitoneum effect

- Compliance change: **-18.7%** (laparoscopic minus open)
- Alveolar CO2 elimination proxy (VCO2): **+32.6%**
- EtCO2 rise: **+4.3 mmHg**
- Untreated MAP rise: **+8.2 mmHg**

Exclusions:

- insufficient pre-incision ventilation data: 22
- missing ventilator tracks: 1
- short surgery under 30 min: 41
